Settings and info

Units

Different clinics use different units. 1 mmol of potassium = 1 mEq = 39 mg. Phosphorus has no mEq value, so it stays in mg.

Why some foods say "no data"

In the United States, food companies are not required to put phosphorus on the label. Potassium was only required starting in 2020.

So most packaged foods have no phosphorus number anywhere. One study of 239,089 packaged foods found phosphorus listed on only 1.45% of them, and potassium on only 5.7%.

Other apps hide this by counting a missing number as zero. That makes your daily total look much lower than it really is. This app shows you what is missing instead.

What we do instead

We read the ingredient list, which is always printed. Added phosphate is absorbed by your body almost completely (about 90% or more), while the phosphorus naturally in food is absorbed at about 40–60%. So knowing an added phosphate is present is useful even without a number.

The common advice is to look for "PHOS" in the ingredients. That misses the two most common ones: lecithin and modified food starch. This app checks for those too, plus potassium salts added to low-sodium foods.

Food data source (optional)

Whole-food numbers come from the USDA. The app works without a key, but the free shared key is slow and limited. Your own key is free from api.data.gov and takes a minute to get.

Your privacy

Your food log is stored only on this device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. We have no account and no server.

When you search for a food, the search words go to the USDA and Open Food Facts to look it up. Nothing about you is sent with them.

Important

This app is a notebook. It is not a medical device and it does not give medical advice.

It will never tell you your limit, warn you that you have had too much, or guess your blood test results. Only your care team can do that, because only they know your labs, your dialysis, and your medicines.

Food data can be wrong or missing. Always check with your dietitian or doctor before changing what you eat.
